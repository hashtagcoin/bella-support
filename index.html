How can we help?

Support for Bella on iOS · Last updated 5 September 2026

Bella is a small team, and a real person reads every email. If your question isn't answered below, write to boredbash@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within five business days.

Getting started

How does Bella build my plan?

During setup you tell Bella your goals, your life stage, the equipment you have, and when you're realistically free. If you choose to share cycle information, Bella also uses that. It then writes a week of sessions around all of it. You can change any of these answers later under Me → Health Profile and Training Preferences, and your plan updates.

Do I have to log my cycle?

No. Every hormone and cycle field is optional and you can skip the whole step. Bella still builds a plan from your goals, schedule and equipment — you just won't get the phase-based adjustments.

What if I have almost no time?

Set your weekly target as low as you like. Ten minutes counts as a session, and so does a walk. Bella is designed around consistency, not heroics.

Your plan and workouts

Why did my workout change?

When you log low energy, poor sleep, symptoms or a late-cycle phase, Bella tapers the load — it reduces weight or reps, slows the tempo, adds rest between sets, or swaps in Pilates or mobility. You can always open the workout library and choose something else instead.

Can I choose my own workout?

Yes. Tap Plan in the bottom bar to browse the full library, or Favourites for anything you've saved.

The exercise cues aren't clear — what do the images mean?

Each movement shows three reference frames: start, middle and finish. Match your position to those, and read the short cue underneath each one. If something hurts, stop and skip the exercise.

Calendar and reminders

Why does Bella want calendar access?

So it can see where you're already busy and suggest sessions in your genuinely free windows. Your events are read on your device and are never uploaded to us. If you decline, everything else in the app still works — you just schedule manually.

How do I turn calendar access off?

iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Bella. You can also turn the feature off inside the app under Me → Calendar.

Your account and data

How do I export my data?

Me tab → Data Export. You get a complete machine-readable copy of everything tied to your account.

How do I delete my account?

Me tab → Delete Account. It is permanent. Full details are on the account deletion page.

I forgot my password.

Self-service password reset isn't in this version yet — it's near the top of our list. For now, email boredbash@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll send you a reset link.

Is my cycle data private?

Yes. Your cycle logs, Apple Health results and body measurements stay on your device. Bella Circle messages are visible only to members of the public room or private group where you post them. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. The privacy policy spells out exactly what we hold and why.

Bella Circle

What happens when I join a circle?

You can join topic rooms, make private interest groups, post messages in those spaces and add friends. Your display name and message are visible to members of that room. Every message can be reported, and you can block another member at any time. AI test participants are clearly labelled and can be hidden.

Troubleshooting

The app won't load my plan

Check your internet connection, then force-quit and reopen Bella. If it persists, sign out under Me → Sign Out and sign back in. Your wellness history stays on this device; account and community records are stored with Supabase. Export your data before uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Where can I send feedback?

Open Me and find the feedback card above Sign Out. We would love to hear your opinion. Feedback is sent privately to our support team, not to the community.

My progress looks wrong

Progress updates when a session is marked complete. If you finished a workout without tapping through to the end, log it manually under the Activity screen.

Bella is not medical advice. It is a fitness and wellbeing app, not a medical device, and it is not a contraceptive or fertility tool. Talk to your doctor or midwife before starting or changing exercise — especially during pregnancy, after birth, or if you have a health condition. If you feel unwell while training, stop.
