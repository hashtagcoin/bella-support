Delete your account

Last updated 15 August 2026

You can permanently delete your Bella account and everything in it from inside the app. You do not need to email us, and you do not need to explain why.

From inside the app

  1. Open Bella and sign in.
  2. Tap Me in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. You'll be signed out straight away.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is removed from our database immediately:

  • Your profile — name, email address and profile photo
  • Your health profile — cycle information, life stage, and any postpartum, pregnancy or injury details you recorded
  • Every hormone and symptom log
  • Your activity history, scheduled sessions, streaks and rewards
  • Your training preferences, goals and notes
  • Your Bella Circle memberships
  • Your consent records and any support requests
  • Your sign-in credentials

Deleting your account also removes anything Bella stored on your device, including Creator Studio playlists.

What this does not touch

  • Events already in your device calendar. If you scheduled workouts to your own calendar, those events belong to you and stay there. Delete them in your calendar app if you don't want them.
  • Encrypted backups. Deleted records roll out of our infrastructure backups within 30 days. They are not accessible or restorable in the meantime.

This cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way for us to restore a deleted account. If you only want a break, sign out instead — your plan and history will be waiting.

If you can't sign in

If you've lost access to your account and can't reach the delete option, email boredbash@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll verify it's you and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Questions before you delete?
Email boredbash@gmail.com. If something in the app isn't working for you, we'd genuinely like to know.
