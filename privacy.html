Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026 · Applies to Bella for iOS, version 1.0

Bella is a fitness and wellbeing app for women. To build a training plan that fits your body, it asks you for some genuinely personal information — including things about your cycle, your energy and your health. This page explains exactly what we hold, why, and how you get rid of it.

The short version. We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics in Bella. You can export or permanently delete everything from inside the app.

Who we are

Bella is published by Bashir Johansson. For anything in this policy, contact boredbash@gmail.com.

What we collect

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Account details Your name and email address, and a securely hashed password. To create your account and sign you in.
Health & cycle information Cycle day and phase, symptoms, flow, mood, energy, motivation and readiness, plus any postpartum, pregnancy or injury status you choose to record. If you connect Apple Health, Bella also reads your daily step count and sleep duration. To adjust workout intensity, exercise selection, tempo and rest. This information is stored on your device and is not uploaded to Bella or Supabase. Bella does not write to Apple Health.

Your notes and preferences Free-text notes on activities and hormone logs, training goals, equipment, location preferences and the circles you have joined. To personalise your plan and remember your setup on this device.
Profile photo An image you pick from your photo library. Optional. Shown on your own profile screen inside the app.
Community and creator content Your community display name, room and private-group memberships, chat messages, friendships, blocks and reports. If you apply to become a creator, your application and the content or social links you submit are also stored. To operate Bella Circle safely, respond to moderation reports, and review creator applications.

Support and feedback

If you choose to send feedback or a support request, Bella sends your message, the request category, your account identifier and the name and email you provide to Supabase. Feedback is categorised separately so our support team can review your opinion and respond. These requests are private and are not published in Bella Circle. Do not include sensitive health information in feedback unless you intend to share it with support. Support records are retained as needed to handle your request and are included in the account-deletion process.

Your device calendar never leaves your device

If you allow calendar access, Bella reads your existing events on your device only, to find free windows where a session could fit. Those events are not uploaded to our servers and are not visible to us. When you choose to schedule a session, Bella writes a single event to your device calendar. You can decline calendar access and the rest of the app works normally.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and Bella contains no ad SDK.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and Bella does not ask for permission to track.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.
  • We do not write to Apple Health. With your permission, Bella reads only step count and sleep duration. We do not read your location or contacts.
  • We do not share your cycle or health information with employers, insurers, advertisers or data brokers.

Who can see your data

Bella Circle is a shared community. Other signed-in members can see your community display name and messages in public rooms. Members of a private group can see the group and its messages. You can report a message and block another member. Blocked members' messages are hidden from you. Reports enter a private moderation queue. AI test participants and their messages are clearly marked in the app and can be hidden.

Supabase provides Bella's account authentication and stores your name, email address, community profiles and messages, memberships, friendships, blocks, reports, creator applications and content-interaction records. Supabase processes that information only to run the service and under contract. Row-level security limits private groups and account-linked records to authorised members. Health and cycle logs, Apple Health results, weight and body measurements, fitness activity, notes, plans, progress, favourites and custom workouts stay in local app storage on your device.

Where your data is stored, and for how long

Account, community, moderation and creator-application records are held in Supabase's managed cloud infrastructure for as long as your account exists or as needed to operate and moderate the service. Local wellness data remains on your device until you clear it or delete your account. Deleting your account clears local Bella data and removes your account-linked cloud records; managed backups roll off within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Export your data. Open Bella, go to the Me tab, then Data Export. You get a machine-readable copy of your Bella account details and locally stored wellness data.
  • Delete your account and data. Open Bella, go to the Me tab, then Delete Account. This is permanent and removes your profile, health information, activity history, notes and rewards. See the deletion page for the full detail.
  • Correct your information. Every field you gave us is editable in the app under Me → Health Profile and Edit Profile.
  • Withdraw permissions. Apple Health, calendar and photo access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

If you are in the EU or UK, these correspond to your GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. If you are in Australia, they correspond to your rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. You can exercise any of them by emailing us, and you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au if you are not satisfied with our response.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Data is encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text and never visible to us. Database row-level security restricts every query to the signed-in account. No system is perfect, so if you believe your account has been accessed without permission, email us immediately.

Children

Bella is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Health information is not medical advice

Bella is a fitness and wellbeing app. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it is not a contraceptive or fertility tool. The cycle and symptom information you enter is used only to adjust exercise recommendations. Always speak to your doctor or midwife before starting or changing exercise, especially during pregnancy, after birth, or if you have a health condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions, requests or complaints
Email boredbash@gmail.com. We aim to reply within five business days, and to complete data access or deletion requests within 30 days.
